Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ShebaPath is an independent guide site for common Bangladesh government services. We are not a government website. This page explains what information we collect when you use ShebaPath and how it's used.

What we collect

If you create an account, we store:

  • Your full name, email address, and phone number (if you provide one)
  • Your password — never in plain text; it is hashed with BCrypt before being stored

We don't collect National ID numbers, passport numbers, payment details, or any other sensitive government document data. ShebaPath only provides step-by-step guides — it does not submit applications to any government office on your behalf.

Cookies

We use a single essential cookie (bd_session) to keep you logged in. It's required for the site to know who you are between page loads and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Translation feature

If you use the "বাংলায় দেখুন" (View in Bangla) button, the page content is translated using Google's website translation widget. This means the page you're viewing is processed by Google Translate. Google's own privacy policy applies to that translation process — we don't control how Google handles that data.

How we use your data

Your account details are used only to let you log in, save your details for future reference, and manage your ShebaPath account. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data storage

Account data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database. We take reasonable steps to keep it secure, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can update your name and phone number anytime from your Account page. If you'd like your account and data deleted entirely, contact us using the details below.
